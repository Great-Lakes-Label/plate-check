Self-check

    Step 1 — Job ticket

    Photograph the job ticket

    Whole ticket in frame, any orientation. The Job # and Item Code barcodes are read automatically.

    Both numbers came from the ticket barcodes. Correct either one if it is wrong, or type it if it was not read.

    Step 2 — Proof

    Photograph the Art Proof Approval

    Whole sheet in frame, flat, glare off the artwork. The Item # is checked against the Product Number in the approval section and the matching artwork is found automatically.

    Step 3 — Press sample

    Photograph one label off the press

    One label, filling the frame, on a dark flat surface. The label is found automatically.
